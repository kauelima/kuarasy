# Future ideas

Things deliberately left out of v1 to keep it small.

## Per-location settings

- Working hours that override the global ones for a single location (a coworker who works 7:00 to 15:00).
- Workdays per location (Sunday to Thursday weeks).

## Time zones

- Custom fixed offsets (`UTC+5:30`) that aren't tied to a city.
- Editing the abbreviation shown for a location by hand.

## Converting

- A quick-convert field that understands "11am EST", "3pm PST tomorrow" and jumps the bar there.

## Calendar

- Public holidays per country, shown as red like the timeanddate converter.
- Add the selected time to Calendar, or copy a short summary ("14:00 São Paulo / 12:00 New York / 09:00 Los Angeles").

## Sync

- iCloud sync of locations and settings (a reason to revisit SwiftData).
